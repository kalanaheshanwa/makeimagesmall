Frequently asked questions

Is WebP better than JPG?

For the web, yes. WebP keeps the same visual quality as JPG while producing a much smaller file, which makes pages load faster and improves SEO. Every modern browser supports WebP.

Will I lose quality converting JPG to WebP?

At the default quality setting the difference is invisible to the eye, while the file gets dramatically smaller. You can raise the quality slider if you want to keep more detail.

Are my JPG files uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device, so it is completely private.

Is there a limit on how many JPGs I can convert?

No limit. Convert as many JPG files as you like, in one batch, for free, with no account.
