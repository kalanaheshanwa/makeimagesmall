What Is Image Compression and How Does It Work?

Image compression is the process of reducing a photo's file size by removing redundant data — either without any visible quality loss (lossless compression) or with a minimal, barely perceptible reduction in quality (lossy compression). For most e-commerce product photos, a well-calibrated lossy compression can reduce file sizes by 60–80% with virtually no noticeable difference to the human eye.

Lossless vs. Lossy Compression for Product Photos

Choosing the right compression method depends on your product type and how your images will be used:

  • Lossless compression is ideal for product images that need to remain pixel-perfect, such as jewelry, electronics with fine text, or technical diagrams. Every detail is preserved.
  • Lossy compression works beautifully for most standard product categories — clothing, furniture, beauty products, food items — where a small reduction in imperceptible data has zero impact on how the product looks to shoppers.

The smartest approach for e-commerce is to use a reliable tool that intelligently balances both methods, delivering the smallest possible file size without compromising your brand's visual standards.

Best Practices for E-Commerce Image Optimization

  • Compress before uploading — always optimize images before adding them to your store, not after.
  • Use the right format — JPEG works well for most product photos; PNG is better for images with transparent backgrounds.
