Recommended Image Sizes for WordPress Blog Posts

Featured Images

Your featured image is the most prominent visual on any blog post. The ideal size for a WordPress featured image is typically 1200 x 628 pixels. This dimension works well for both your website layout and social media sharing, especially on platforms like Facebook and LinkedIn. Keep the file size under 200KB whenever possible.

In-Content Images

Images placed inside your blog post content should generally be no wider than 800 pixels. Most WordPress themes have a content area that's between 700 and 900 pixels wide. Uploading a 4000-pixel-wide image to fill a 800-pixel space is a massive waste of bandwidth and storage. Always resize your images to match your content column width before uploading.

Thumbnail Images

WordPress automatically generates thumbnail versions of your images for use in category pages, widgets, and related post sections. The default WordPress thumbnail size is 150 x 150 pixels. You can customize these sizes under Settings > Media in your WordPress dashboard to match your specific theme requirements.

Best File Formats for WordPress Images

Choosing the right file format is just as important as choosing the right dimensions. Here's a quick breakdown:

  • JPEG/JPG – Best for photographs and complex images with lots of color. Offers excellent compression with minimal quality loss.
  • PNG – Best for images that require transparent backgrounds, logos, or screenshots with sharp text.
  • WebP – A modern format supported by most browsers that delivers 25–35% smaller file sizes than JPEG or PNG with comparable quality.
  • GIF – Only use for simple animations. Avoid for photographs as file sizes are large and quality is poor.

Whenever possible, convert your images to WebP format before uploading. This single change can significantly improve your site speed without sacrificing visual quality.
