Why Image Optimization Matters for Squarespace

Squarespace does offer some built-in image handling, including lazy loading and responsive image delivery. However, it does not automatically compress your images before they are stored and served. This means if you upload a 6MB JPEG directly from your camera, Squarespace will serve a slightly resized version of that bloated file to your visitors — still causing significant slowdowns.

Google's Core Web Vitals — the metrics that directly influence your SEO rankings — measure things like Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), which tracks how quickly the main content of your page loads. Unoptimized images are one of the leading causes of poor LCP scores. If your Squarespace website is loaded with large, uncompressed images, you are almost certainly leaving both speed and SEO performance on the table.

How to Optimize Images for Your Squarespace Website

Optimizing images for Squarespace does not require technical expertise or expensive software. The most effective approach is to compress and resize your images before uploading them to your Squarespace site. This gives you complete control over file sizes without relying on any platform-level processing.

Step 1: Choose the Right File Format

Before you compress, make sure you are using the right format. JPEG is ideal for photographs and complex images with lots of color. PNG works best for graphics, logos, and images that require transparency. WebP is the modern format of choice — it delivers superior compression while maintaining visual quality and is now widely supported across all major browsers.

Step 2: Resize Images to the Correct Dimensions

Squarespace templates have maximum content widths — typically between 1500px and 2500px wide. There is absolutely no reason to upload an image that is 5000px wide when your template will never display it at that size. Resize your images to match the maximum display size before uploading. This simple step alone can reduce your file sizes by 50% or more.

Best Practices for Squarespace Image SEO

Compressing your images is the foundation, but there are additional steps that will supercharge your Squarespace SEO performance.

Write Descriptive Alt Text

Every image on your Squarespace site should have meaningful alt text. Alt text helps search engines understand what your image depicts, which contributes to your overall page relevance for target keywords. It also makes your site accessible to visually impaired users.

Use Descriptive File Names

Before uploading, rename your image files with descriptive, keyword-relevant names. Instead of IMG_4892.jpg, use something like squarespace-portfolio-homepage-banner.jpg. Search engine crawlers read file names as part of their image indexing process.
