Why Image Size Matters on Social Media

Every major social media platform has its own image size requirements and file upload limits. When you upload an oversized image, a few things can go wrong:

  • Slow upload times that frustrate both you and your audience
  • Automatic compression by the platform, which often results in blurry or pixelated visuals
  • Longer page load times for followers viewing your content on mobile
  • Reduced engagement because poor-quality images get scrolled past

By taking control of your image compression before you upload, you ensure your visuals look sharp, load fast, and make the right impression every single time.

Recommended Image Sizes for Popular Social Media Platforms

Instagram

Instagram supports square (1080×1080px), portrait (1080×1350px), and landscape (1080×566px) images. Keep file sizes under 8MB, but ideally aim for under 1MB for faster loading and better quality control.

Facebook

For Facebook posts, the recommended image size is 1200×630px. Facebook will automatically compress anything too large, so getting ahead of that process yourself always produces better results.

Twitter / X

Twitter recommends images at 1200×675px with a maximum file size of 5MB for photos. Keeping your files lean helps tweets load instantly for your followers.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn post images look best at 1200×627px. For professional content, crisp and fast-loading images are especially important to maintain credibility.

Tips for Getting the Best Results When Compressing Social Media Images

Start with the Highest Quality Original

Always compress from the original high-resolution file. Compressing an already-compressed image leads to a compounding loss of quality that you won't be able to recover.

Choose the Right File Format

For photos with lots of color variation, JPG is your best bet. For images with text, logos, or transparent backgrounds, use PNG. If your platform supports it, WebP offers the best compression with excellent quality.

Don't Over-Compress

While it's tempting to compress as much as possible, aim for a balance. Most tools that let you compress image online free offer a quality slider — keeping it between 70–85% typically delivers a great balance of small file size and visual clarity.

Resize Before You Compress

If your image dimensions are much larger than what the platform requires, resize it to the correct dimensions first. This alone can dramatically reduce file size before you even apply compression.
