The Science Behind Image Compression for E-Commerce

Understanding Lossy vs. Lossless Compression

There are two main approaches to image compression, and understanding both helps you make smarter decisions for your product catalog.

  • Lossy compression — Reduces file size by permanently removing some image data. When done well, the quality loss is imperceptible to the human eye. This method delivers the most dramatic file size reductions, often shrinking images by 60–80%.
  • Lossless compression — Reduces file size without discarding any data. The original image quality is perfectly preserved, though file size reductions are more modest.

For most e-commerce product photos, a high-quality lossy compression strikes the perfect balance between visual appeal and performance. Your product images still look crisp and professional while loading significantly faster.

Choosing the Right File Format

Format selection plays a critical role in how efficiently your images compress. Here's a quick guide:

  • JPEG — Ideal for detailed product photography with complex color gradients
  • PNG — Best for product images requiring transparent backgrounds
  • WebP — A modern format offering superior compression with excellent quality, supported by all major browsers

Whenever possible, converting product images to WebP format can reduce file sizes by an additional 25–34% compared to JPEG, without any noticeable quality degradation.

Best Practices for Compressing E-Commerce Product Images

  • Compress before uploading — Always optimize images before adding them to your store platform
  • Aim for under 200KB per image — This is a solid benchmark for most product photos
  • Maintain consistent dimensions — Resize images to your theme's actual display dimensions before compressing
  • Use descriptive filenames — SEO-friendly filenames help your images rank in Google Image Search
  • Add alt text — Descriptive alt tags improve accessibility and SEO
  • Batch compress your catalog — Don't just optimize new images; revisit your existing product library
