Why Image File Size Limits Matter

Platform image limits aren't arbitrary. They exist to protect server resources, ensure fast loading speeds, and deliver a consistent experience to all users. When your image exceeds these limits, platforms either reject your upload outright or automatically compress it — often with ugly, visible quality loss.

The smarter approach is to take control of your own image compression before you upload. That way, you decide where quality is preserved and where file size is trimmed.

Social Media Image Size Limits (2024)

Facebook

  • Profile photo: Max 100KB recommended for sharpest display
  • Cover photo: Under 100KB, JPG or PNG
  • Feed images: Max file size 4MB (PNG), 4MB (JPG)
  • Stories: Max 4MB for images

Facebook automatically recompresses any image over its internal thresholds, which can make your photos look washed out. Pre-compressing before upload gives you much better results.

Instagram

  • Feed posts: Max 8MB per image
  • Stories and Reels thumbnails: Max 30MB
  • Recommended dimensions: 1080 x 1080px (square), 1080 x 1350px (portrait)

Instagram is notorious for crushing image quality on slower connections. Keeping your file size under 1MB while maintaining 1080px width is the sweet spot for sharp, fast-loading posts.

Twitter / X

  • Profile photo: Max 2MB, JPG, GIF, or PNG
  • Header image: Max 5MB
  • Tweet images: Max 5MB (JPG/PNG), 15MB (GIF via web)

LinkedIn

  • Profile photo: Max 8MB
  • Cover image: Max 8MB
  • Post images: Max 5MB per image
  • Article images: Max 5MB

Pinterest

  • Pin images: Max 20MB
  • Recommended format: JPG or PNG
  • Ideal aspect ratio: 2:3 (1000 x 1500px)

TikTok

  • Profile photo: Max 50KB (surprisingly small!)
  • Photo posts: Max 20MB per image

Website & CMS Platform Limits

WordPress

  • Default upload limit: 2MB (can be increased by hosting)
  • Recommended image size: Under 500KB for optimal page speed
  • Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP

WordPress page speed scores depend heavily on image weight. Google's Core Web Vitals penalize slow-loading pages, so keeping images compressed is essential for SEO.

Shopify

  • Product images: Max 20MB per image
  • Recommended: Under 1MB for fast storefront loading
  • Max dimensions: 4472 x 4472px

Squarespace

  • Standard images: Max 20MB
  • Best practice: Under 500KB for smooth page performance

Wix

  • Uploaded images: Max 25MB per file
  • Recommended: Under 1MB for fast rendering

Email & Other Platforms

Gmail & Email Clients

  • Inline images: Under 10MB total attachment size recommended
  • Email newsletter images: Keep individual images under 200KB to avoid slow loads on mobile

Google Business Profile

  • Photos: Between 10KB and 5MB
  • Minimum dimensions: 720 x 720px

YouTube

  • Custom thumbnail: Max 2MB, JPG, GIF, or PNG
  • Recommended size: 1280 x 720px
