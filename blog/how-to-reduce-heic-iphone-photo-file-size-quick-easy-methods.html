If you own an iPhone, you've probably noticed that your photos take up a surprising amount of storage space. Apple's default HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) format is designed to save space compared to JPEG, but HEIC files can still be surprisingly large — especially when you're shooting in high resolution or burst mode. Whether you're trying to free up phone storage, send photos by email, or upload images to a website, reducing your HEIC photo file size is an essential skill every iPhone user should know.

In this guide, we'll walk you through the quickest and easiest methods to shrink your iPhone photos without sacrificing too much quality. Let's dive in.

Why Are HEIC Files So Large?

HEIC is Apple's version of the HEIF (High Efficiency Image Format) standard. While it's more efficient than JPEG in many ways — offering better quality at roughly half the file size — modern iPhones shoot at such high resolutions (up to 48MP on newer models) that even HEIC files can easily reach 5–15MB per photo.

When you multiply that by hundreds or thousands of photos, you're looking at gigabytes of storage eaten up fast. The good news? There are several reliable ways to compress and reduce that file size quickly.

Method 2: Change iPhone Camera Settings to Shoot in JPEG

If you want to prevent large HEIC files from being created in the first place, you can simply change your iPhone camera settings to capture in JPEG format instead.

Steps to Switch to JPEG on iPhone

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Scroll down and tap Camera
  • Tap Formats
  • Select Most Compatible instead of High Efficiency

From this point forward, your camera will capture photos in JPEG format, which is universally compatible with all devices and platforms. The trade-off is that JPEG files may be slightly larger than HEIC files, but they'll be much easier to share, upload, and compress using standard tools.

Method 3: Convert and Compress HEIC Files on Your Mac

If you're a Mac user, you can use the built-in Preview app to convert and resize HEIC photos without installing anything extra.

Steps to Compress HEIC with Preview

  • Open the HEIC file in Preview
  • Go to File > Export
  • Choose JPEG from the Format dropdown menu
  • Adjust the Quality slider to reduce file size
  • Click Save

This is a solid method for desktop users, but if you're on Windows or working directly from your iPhone, an online compressor is the better route.

Method 4: Resize the Image Resolution

Another effective way to reduce file size is to lower the image resolution. Most social media platforms and websites don't require 4K or 48MP photos — a 1080px or 2000px wide image is more than sufficient for most uses.
