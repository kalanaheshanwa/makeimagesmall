Why Image Compression Matters for Shopify Stores

Shopify stores are visual by nature. Most merchants upload high-resolution photos straight from a camera or stock photo website without thinking twice about file size. A single product image can easily be 5–10 MB, and if your product page has ten images, you're looking at up to 100 MB of data that a customer's browser needs to download before they can even scroll through your products.

Here's what slow images cost you:

  • Higher bounce rates: Studies show that 53% of mobile users abandon a site that takes more than 3 seconds to load.
  • Lower Google rankings: Page speed is a confirmed Google ranking factor, affecting both desktop and mobile SEO.
  • Lost sales: A one-second delay in page load time can reduce conversions by up to 7%.
  • Poor user experience: Shoppers expect fast, seamless browsing — lagging pages send them straight to your competitors.

Compressing your images solves all of these problems at once without sacrificing the visual quality your customers expect.

What Is Image Compression?

Image compression is the process of reducing a file's size by removing unnecessary data. There are two main types:

  • Lossy compression: Slightly reduces image quality to achieve much smaller file sizes. Great for product photos where a minor quality reduction is invisible to the human eye.
  • Lossless compression: Reduces file size without any quality loss. Ideal for logos, icons, and graphics with text.

For most Shopify product images, lossy compression at 70–85% quality delivers the perfect balance between visual sharpness and fast load speed.

Best Image Formats for Shopify

JPEG

JPEG is the go-to format for product photography. It supports millions of colors and compresses beautifully for real-world images like clothing, furniture, electronics, and food.

PNG

Use PNG for images that need a transparent background, such as logos or product cutouts. PNG files tend to be larger, so compression is especially important here.

WebP

WebP is a modern format supported by all major browsers and offers 25–35% smaller file sizes than JPEG at equivalent quality. If possible, convert your images to WebP for the best performance.

Recommended Image Sizes for Shopify

Shopify recommends keeping product images under 20 MB, but for real-world performance, you should target much smaller sizes:

  • Product images: 800 x 800 px to 2048 x 2048 px, under 200 KB
  • Collection banners: 1200 x 400 px, under 300 KB
  • Blog images: 1200 x 630 px, under 250 KB
  • Logos: Under 50 KB whenever possible
