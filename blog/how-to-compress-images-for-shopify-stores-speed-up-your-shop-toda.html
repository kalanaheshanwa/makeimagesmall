What Is Image Compression and How Does It Work?

Image compression is the process of reducing a file's size by removing unnecessary data. There are two types: lossless compression, which maintains 100% of the original quality, and lossy compression, which removes some data to achieve much smaller file sizes while keeping the image looking virtually identical to the human eye.

For Shopify product images, a combination of both techniques usually works best. You want your photos to look crisp and professional, but you also want them to load in under two seconds. A good image compression tool finds that perfect balance automatically, saving you hours of manual editing.

Best Image Formats for Shopify

JPEG for Product Photos

JPEG is the go-to format for product photography. It handles color gradients and complex images beautifully while compressing to relatively small file sizes. Use JPEG for lifestyle shots, product detail images, and banner photos.

PNG for Logos and Graphics

PNG supports transparency, making it ideal for logos, icons, and graphics that need to sit on different colored backgrounds. PNG files are typically larger than JPEGs, which makes compression even more critical for this format.

WebP for Modern Performance

WebP is a newer format supported by most modern browsers. It delivers smaller file sizes than both JPEG and PNG while maintaining excellent quality. Shopify has added native WebP support, making it a smart choice for performance-focused stores.
