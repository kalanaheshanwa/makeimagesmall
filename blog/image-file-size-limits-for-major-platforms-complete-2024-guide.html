Social Media Platform Image Size Limits

Instagram

  • Maximum file size: 8 MB for photos
  • Recommended formats: JPEG, PNG
  • Profile picture: Under 1 MB recommended
  • Stories and Reels thumbnails: Keep under 4 MB for best quality

Instagram automatically recompresses anything over its internal thresholds, often resulting in noticeably degraded quality. Upload pre-optimized images to keep full control over how your content looks.

Facebook

  • Maximum file size: 15 MB per photo
  • Cover photos: Under 2 MB strongly recommended
  • Ads images: Must be under 30 MB, ideally under 1 MB
  • Recommended format: JPEG for photos, PNG for logos/graphics

Facebook's ad platform is especially strict. Oversized ad images can slow down your campaign delivery and increase your cost per click. Always optimize before running paid campaigns.

Twitter / X

  • Maximum file size: 5 MB for photos, 15 MB for GIFs
  • Profile image: Under 2 MB
  • Banner image: Under 5 MB
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP

LinkedIn

  • Profile photo: Maximum 8 MB
  • Banner image: Maximum 8 MB
  • Post images: Up to 5 MB per image, up to 20 images per post
  • Article cover image: Under 10 MB

LinkedIn serves a professional audience where image quality directly reflects your brand credibility. Blurry or pixelated images hurt professional perception. Compress smartly — reduce file size without sacrificing visual sharpness.

TikTok

  • Profile picture: Under 1 MB recommended
  • Photo post images: Under 5 MB each
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP

E-Commerce Platform Image Requirements

Amazon

  • Maximum file size: 10 MB per image
  • Minimum resolution: 1000 pixels on the longest side
  • Recommended size: 2000 x 2000 pixels for zoom capability
  • Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, TIFF, GIF

Amazon requires high-resolution images to enable the zoom feature, which has been proven to increase conversions. The challenge is balancing quality with file size. You want detail without sluggish load times on your product pages.

Etsy

  • Maximum file size: 1 MB per listing image
  • Recommended dimensions: 2000 x 2000 pixels
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF

Etsy's 1 MB limit is one of the strictest among e-commerce platforms. You'll almost certainly need to compress image online free before uploading product photos if you're shooting with a modern DSLR or smartphone.

Shopify

  • Maximum file size: 20 MB per image
  • Recommended format: JPEG for product photos, PNG for transparent backgrounds
  • Ideal dimensions: 2048 x 2048 pixels for square images

Website and CMS Platform Limits

WordPress

  • Default upload limit: 2 MB (can be increased by hosting providers)
  • Recommended image size for blog posts: Under 200 KB
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG (with plugins)

WordPress sites suffer significantly from unoptimized images. A single hero image over 1 MB can add seconds to your page load time. Most SEO experts recommend keeping individual images under 150–200 KB for optimal Core Web Vitals scores.
