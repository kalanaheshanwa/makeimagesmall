Why Image File Size Limits Matter

Platforms enforce file size limits for good reason. Large images slow down page load speeds, consume excessive server storage, and create friction in the user experience. When you upload an oversized image, platforms either reject it outright or apply their own aggressive compression — often resulting in a pixelated, low-quality mess. Taking control of your image compression before uploading ensures you always get the best visual quality within allowed limits.

Social Media Platform Image Size Limits (2024)

Facebook

  • Profile photo: Maximum 5 MB
  • Cover photo: Maximum 5 MB, recommended 820 x 312 px (JPEG or PNG)
  • Shared images in posts: Maximum 15 MB per photo
  • Stories: Maximum 4 MB for images

Facebook automatically recompresses images that exceed its quality thresholds, so uploading an already-optimized image leads to significantly better results on your timeline and ads.

Instagram

  • Feed photos: Maximum 8 MB
  • Stories: Maximum 30 MB
  • Reels thumbnails: Maximum 8 MB
  • Recommended format: JPEG with sRGB color space

Instagram's internal compression is notoriously aggressive. Uploading a pre-compressed, high-quality JPEG between 1–3 MB typically delivers the sharpest results on the platform.

Twitter / X

  • Profile image: Maximum 2 MB
  • Header image: Maximum 5 MB
  • Tweet images: Maximum 5 MB for JPEG/PNG, 15 MB for GIF

LinkedIn

  • Profile photo: Maximum 8 MB
  • Background/cover image: Maximum 8 MB
  • Post images: Maximum 5 MB per image
  • Article cover images: Maximum 10 MB

Pinterest

  • Pins: Maximum 20 MB for JPEG/PNG, 32 MB for GIF
  • Profile photo: Maximum 10 MB
  • Board cover: Maximum 10 MB

E-Commerce Platform Image Size Limits (2024)

Shopify

  • Product images: Maximum 20 MB, recommended under 2 MB for performance
  • Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP
  • Maximum dimensions: 4472 x 4472 px

Etsy

  • Listing photos: Maximum 1 MB per image
  • Recommended resolution: At least 2000 px on the shortest side
  • Accepted formats: JPEG, GIF, PNG

Etsy's 1 MB limit is one of the tightest in the industry. If you're a seller, you'll need to compress image online free regularly to keep your listings looking sharp without hitting upload errors.

Amazon Seller Central

  • Product images: Maximum 10 MB
  • Minimum dimensions: 1000 px on the longest side (for zoom feature)
  • Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, TIFF

Website and CMS Platform Limits (2024)

WordPress

  • Default maximum upload size: 2 MB (varies by hosting provider, often up to 64 MB)
  • Recommended image size: Under 200 KB for optimal page speed
  • Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP

Squarespace

  • Maximum file size: 20 MB per image
  • Recommended: Under 500 KB for blog or gallery images

Wix

  • Maximum file size: 25 MB per image
  • Recommended: Under 2 MB for all images used in site design

Email Marketing Image Size Limits (2024)

  • Mailchimp: Maximum 10 MB per image file
  • Klaviyo: Maximum 5 MB per image
  • Constant Contact: Maximum 5 MB per image
  • Best practice: Keep email images under 200 KB to ensure fast loading in inboxes
