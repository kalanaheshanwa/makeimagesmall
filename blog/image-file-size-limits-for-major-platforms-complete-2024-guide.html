Why Image File Size Limits Matter

Platforms enforce file size limits for good reason. Large image files slow down page load speeds, consume server resources, and create poor user experiences on mobile devices. When you exceed a platform's limit, one of three things happens: your upload is rejected, the platform auto-compresses your image (often poorly), or your page performance tanks.

The smart move is always to compress image online free using a reliable tool before you ever hit upload. This gives you full control over quality and file size rather than letting an algorithm make that decision for you.

Social Media Platform Image Size Limits

Instagram

  • Feed Photos: Maximum 8 MB per image
  • Stories: Maximum 4 MB for photos
  • Reels Thumbnails: Keep under 5 MB for best results
  • Recommended format: JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics

Instagram applies its own compression to any image you upload, which can result in blurry or washed-out visuals. Pre-compressing your images gives you more control over how the final result looks.

Facebook

  • Profile Pictures: Maximum 15 MB
  • Cover Photos: Maximum 100 MB (but keep under 1 MB for fast loading)
  • Feed Images: Maximum 4 MB per photo
  • Facebook Ads: Maximum 30 MB

Twitter / X

  • Single Image Tweets: Maximum 5 MB for JPEGs and PNGs
  • GIFs: Maximum 15 MB on mobile, 15 MB on web
  • Profile Photos: Maximum 2 MB
  • Header Images: Maximum 5 MB

LinkedIn

  • Profile Photo: Maximum 8 MB
  • Cover Image: Maximum 8 MB
  • Post Images: Maximum 5 MB per image
  • Article Cover Images: Maximum 10 MB

Pinterest

  • Standard Pins: Maximum 32 MB
  • Recommended: Keep under 1 MB for faster load speeds
  • Story Pins: Maximum 20 MB per image

TikTok

  • Profile Photos: Maximum 2 MB
  • In-Feed Ad Images: Maximum 500 KB

Website and CMS Platform Limits

WordPress

  • Default Maximum Upload Size: 2 MB (can be increased by host)
  • Recommended: Keep images under 200 KB for optimal page speed
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP

WordPress page speed scores suffer dramatically when images are too large. Running your visuals through a tool to compress image online free before uploading to WordPress is considered best practice by most web developers.

Shopify

  • Product Images: Maximum 20 MB per image
  • Recommended: Under 500 KB for fast storefront loading
  • Maximum Dimensions: 4472 x 4472 pixels

Squarespace

  • Images: Maximum 20 MB per upload
  • Recommended: Keep under 500 KB for performance

Wix

  • Maximum Upload: 25 MB per image
  • Recommended: Under 1 MB for gallery images

Email Platform Image Size Limits

Mailchimp, Klaviyo, and Most Email Clients

  • Maximum File Size: 1 MB per image is the safe standard
  • Recommended: Under 200 KB for fast email load times
  • Total Email Size: Keep entire email under 100 KB of HTML

Email images that are too large can trigger spam filters and cause images to not render at all in certain clients. Always optimize before you send.
