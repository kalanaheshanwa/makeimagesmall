Every platform has its rules. Whether you're uploading a profile photo to LinkedIn, sharing a product image on Shopify, or posting a reel thumbnail on Instagram, file size limits and dimension requirements can make or break your content. Exceeding these limits leads to rejected uploads, blurry compression artifacts, or painfully slow load times that drive users away. Why Image File Size Limits Matter

Platform file size limits exist for good reason. They protect server bandwidth, ensure fast load times, and create a consistent experience for all users. When your image exceeds the allowed size, platforms either reject the upload outright or automatically compress it — usually with poor results. That automatic compression often introduces blurring, color shifts, and visible artifacts that make your content look unprofessional.

The smarter approach is to compress your images before uploading, giving you full control over quality. Using a tool to compress image online free lets you reduce file size without sacrificing the visual quality that matters to your audience.

Social Media Platform Image Size Limits

Instagram

  • Feed photos: Maximum 8 MB per image
  • Stories: Maximum 30 MB
  • Profile picture: Recommended under 200 KB for fast loading
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP

Instagram recompresses every image it receives. Uploading a high-quality, pre-optimized JPEG under 1 MB gives you the best results after Instagram applies its own processing.

Facebook

  • Timeline photos: Maximum 15 MB
  • Cover photo: Under 100 KB recommended for fastest display
  • Shared link thumbnails: Minimum 200 x 200 pixels
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, TIFF

Twitter (X)

  • Photos: Maximum 5 MB
  • GIFs: Maximum 15 MB on desktop, 5 MB on mobile
  • Profile photo: Maximum 2 MB
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP

LinkedIn

  • Post images: Maximum 10 MB
  • Profile photo: Between 10 KB and 8 MB
  • Company logo: Maximum 4 MB
  • Background photo: Maximum 8 MB

Pinterest

  • Pins: Maximum 20 MB
  • Ideal pin size: Under 1 MB for fast loading in feeds
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, BMP

E-Commerce Platform Image Requirements

Shopify

  • Product images: Maximum 20 MB per image
  • Recommended upload size: Under 500 KB for optimal store speed
  • Maximum dimensions: 4472 x 4472 pixels
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP

Store speed directly affects your conversion rate on Shopify. Research consistently shows that a one-second delay in page load time reduces conversions by up to 7%. Keeping product images under 200 KB is a best practice for high-performing stores.

Etsy

  • Listing photos: Maximum 1 MB per image
  • Recommended dimensions: 2000 x 2000 pixels minimum
  • Supported formats: JPEG, GIF, PNG

Amazon

  • Main product image: Maximum 10 MB
  • Recommended size: Under 1 MB for best performance
  • Minimum dimensions: 1000 pixels on the longest side for zoom
  • Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, TIFF

Website and CMS Platform Limits

WordPress

  • Default upload limit: 2 MB (adjustable by host)
  • Recommended image size: Under 150 KB for blog images
  • Featured images: Under 100 KB for fastest page loads

Squarespace and Wix

  • Maximum file size: 20 MB per image on both platforms
  • Recommended upload: Under 500 KB for optimal performance
  • Best format: JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency

Email Marketing Image Limits

Email clients are notoriously unforgiving with large images. Most email marketing best practices recommend keeping every image in your email under 1 MB, with the total email size staying below 100 KB when possible. Large images trigger spam filters and load slowly on mobile devices.

  • Mailchimp: Maximum 1 MB per image, 10 MB total storage per upload session
  • Klaviyo: Maximum 5 MB per image
  • Campaign Monitor: Maximum 5 MB per image
