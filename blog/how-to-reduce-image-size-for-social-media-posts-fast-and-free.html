Ideal Image Sizes for Major Social Media Platforms

Instagram

Instagram recommends keeping image files under 8MB, but for best performance, aim for under 1MB. Square posts look best at 1080 x 1080 pixels, while Stories should be 1080 x 1920 pixels.

Facebook

Facebook automatically compresses anything over 100KB for feed posts, which can destroy quality. Upload images already optimized to around 80–100KB at 1200 x 630 pixels for the best results.

Twitter / X

Twitter supports images up to 5MB for photos, but compressed images under 1MB load noticeably faster in timelines and perform better on mobile.

LinkedIn

LinkedIn works best with images under 5MB, but a well-compressed image at 300–500KB will load faster and still appear sharp on professional feeds.

Pinterest

Pinterest images should be under 20MB, but vertical images at 1000 x 1500 pixels compressed to 500KB or less perform the best in terms of load speed and pin quality.

Tips to Maintain Quality While Reducing File Size

Compression doesn't have to mean blurry or pixelated photos. Follow these best practices to keep your images looking great:

  • Use JPEG for photos — JPEG compression is highly efficient for real-world photography
  • Use PNG for graphics and logos — PNG preserves sharp edges and text
  • Use WebP when possible — WebP offers superior compression with excellent quality retention
  • Avoid over-compressing — dropping quality below 60% often causes noticeable artifacts
  • Resize before compressing — scaling an image to its intended display size removes unnecessary pixel data
  • Always keep your original file as a backup before compressing
