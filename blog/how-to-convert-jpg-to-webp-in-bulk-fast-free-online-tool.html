What Is WebP and Why Should You Use It?

WebP is a modern image format developed by Google specifically for the web. It delivers significantly smaller file sizes compared to JPG and PNG while maintaining excellent visual quality. In fact, WebP images are typically 25% to 35% smaller than equivalent JPG files, sometimes even more.

Key Benefits of WebP Format

  • Smaller file sizes – Reduces page load time dramatically
  • Lossless and lossy compression – You choose the quality level that works for you
  • Transparency support – Unlike JPG, WebP supports alpha channels
  • Better SEO performance – Faster pages rank higher on Google
  • Wide browser support – Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge all support WebP natively

Google's own PageSpeed Insights tool actively recommends serving images in WebP format. If you're still uploading raw JPG files to your website, you're leaving a lot of performance on the table.

Tips for Getting the Best Results

Choose the Right Quality Level

WebP allows you to fine-tune the compression ratio. For most web images, a quality setting of 80% delivers visually lossless results at a fraction of the original file size. If you're converting product images for an e-commerce site, you might want to stay closer to 85% to preserve fine details.

Always Keep Your Originals

Before you convert and replace your image library, keep a backup of your original JPG files. Conversion is essentially a one-way process, and you may need the originals later for print or other high-resolution purposes.

Compress Before Uploading

Even after converting to WebP, make sure your images are sized correctly for the web. Uploading a 4000px wide image when your website only displays at 1200px is wasteful. When you compress image online free, also resize your images to match their display dimensions.
