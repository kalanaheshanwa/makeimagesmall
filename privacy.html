Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your images stay on your device

MakeImageSmall processes all images entirely inside your browser using the Canvas API. No images are ever uploaded to our servers. We cannot see, access, or store any image you compress using this tool.

What we collect

We use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymous usage statistics — page views, session duration, country, and device type. This data does not include any images or personal identifiers. You can opt out using a browser ad blocker or the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Cookies

This site uses cookies only for Google Analytics and Google AdSense functionality. We do not use any tracking cookies of our own.

Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: privacy@makeimagesmall.com
