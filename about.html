About MakeImageSmall

A free, private image compressor built for everyone — from web developers to iPhone users who just want smaller photos.

🔒 Your images never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser using the Canvas API. Zero uploads. Zero servers. Zero privacy risk.

Why we built this

Most image compression tools upload your files to a server, process them, and send them back. That's slow, requires an internet connection for the actual compression, and means your private photos pass through someone else's servers.

We built MakeImageSmall differently. The entire compression happens inside your browser — the same technology that powers Google's Squoosh, but simpler and faster to use with no setup required.

How it works

When you drop an image, your browser reads it, draws it onto an invisible canvas, and re-encodes it as WebP — Google's modern image format that achieves 25–80% smaller file sizes than JPG or PNG at the same visual quality. The result is a new file created entirely on your device.

We support JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, AVIF, TIFF, WebP, and HEIC/HEIF (iPhone photos). All formats compress to WebP for maximum size reduction.
