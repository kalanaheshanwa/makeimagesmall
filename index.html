Frequently asked questions

How does this image compressor work?
This tool uses your browser's Canvas API to re-encode images into WebP — Google's modern image format built for the web. WebP achieves 25–80% smaller file sizes compared to JPG and PNG at the same visual quality.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. Everything runs inside your browser. Your images are never sent anywhere — complete privacy, and faster processing since there's no upload time.
What image formats are supported?
JPG/JPEG, PNG, GIF, BMP, AVIF, TIFF, WebP, and HEIC/HEIF (iPhone photos). All files are converted to WebP for maximum compression.
How much will it reduce my file size?
Typical results: JPG compresses 30–50%, PNG compresses 50–80%, GIF compresses 40–60%, and iPhone HEIC photos often compress 60–80%. Results vary by image content and quality setting.
Will images lose quality after compression?
At the default quality of 82 the difference is invisible to the human eye. Use the Preview button after compression to compare original vs compressed side-by-side before downloading.
Is there a limit on how many images I can compress?
No limit at all. Compress as many images as you need in one batch, for free, forever. No account, no sign-up, no restrictions of any kind.
Why use WebP instead of JPG or PNG?
WebP is supported by all modern browsers and provides significantly better compression. Smaller images means faster websites, better Google Core Web Vitals, and lower bandwidth costs.
Is this tool really free?
Yes, completely free — no subscription, no account, no watermarks, no limits. This tool is supported by ads which keeps it free for everyone.
