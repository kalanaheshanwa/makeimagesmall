Frequently asked questions

How many images can I compress at once?

As many as you like. There is no file count limit — add a whole folder and compress them all together.

Can I download them all together?

Yes. Once the batch is done, the “Download all” button packages every compressed image into one ZIP.

Will a large batch slow down my computer?

Because the work happens in your browser, very large batches use your device’s memory. If you have hundreds of files, compressing them in a few groups is smoother.

Are the files uploaded?

No. Batch compression runs entirely on your device, so nothing is sent to a server.
